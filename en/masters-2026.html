Hasselblad Masters 2026
Finalist / Project//21

In April 2026, this project was selected among the top finalists of the Hasselblad Masters 2026. It is one of the world's most prestigious photography competitions, receiving over 108,000 submissions from photographers across more than 160 countries.

The Project//21 category is open exclusively to photographers aged 21 and younger. With no restrictions on theme or genre, it is an open category where young photographers can define their own visual language. Ten finalists were selected globally in this category.

The series presented here documents the Finnish landscape after dark in early winter, when there is little snow on the ground and darkness takes over the scene, photographed in 2025.
