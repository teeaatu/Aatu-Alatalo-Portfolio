Hasselblad Masters 2026
Finalist / Project//21

In April 2026, this project was selected among the top finalists of the Hasselblad Masters 2026 — one of the world's most prestigious photography competitions — receiving over 108,000 submissions from photographers across more than 160 countries.

The Project//21 category is open exclusively to photographers aged 21 and younger. With no restrictions on theme or genre, it is an unconstrained platform for young photographers to define their own visual language. Ten finalists were selected globally in this category.

The Project//21 category winner was Panitbhand Paribatra Na Ayudhya (Thailand) for his series Dwellers of the Night — vibrant underwater photographs documenting pelagic marine life in the night waters of the Philippines.

The series presented here documents the Finnish winter landscape after dark — scenes that move along the edge of reality and dream, photographed in 2025.
