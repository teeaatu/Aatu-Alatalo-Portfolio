Aatu Alatalo

Photographer Oulu & Hasselblad Masters Finalist

I am a 21-year-old photographer from the Tyrnävä Lakeus region, currently based in Oulu. I study media at Oulu University of Applied Sciences. I started photography in 2014, and over the past decade, my camera has become an inseparable part of my everyday life.

Awards and Recognitions

  • Hasselblad Masters 2026: Finalist in Project//21 category.
  • SAKUstars 2023: Honorable mention in "Photographic Art in a Day".

Selected Projects

  • Outshine: Digital advertising project for Oulu bus stops 2025 and 2026.
  • Exhibitions: Exhibited at Piippolan Könkkölä and Kestilä Library 2023–2024.
